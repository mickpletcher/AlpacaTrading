# Support

AlpacaTrading is a community-maintained learning toolkit, not a brokerage, investment adviser, or maintainer-operated hosted service. Maintainers cannot access or recover your Alpaca account. No response deadline or trading outcome is promised.

## Getting started

Start with the [root setup guide](README.md), then the component guide in the [documentation map](README.md#documentation-map). Read [known defects](ISSUES.md) and [operations and recovery](OPERATIONS.md) before running a paper-order tool. All order paths must remain paper-only.

## Where to ask

- **Usage questions:** use the [question form](https://github.com/mickpletcher/AlpacaTrading/issues/new?template=question.yml).
- **Reproducible bugs:** use the [bug report](https://github.com/mickpletcher/AlpacaTrading/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include a minimal synthetic example and exact error text after removing secrets.
- **Documentation and ideas:** use the [issue chooser](https://github.com/mickpletcher/AlpacaTrading/issues/new/choose).
- **Accessibility:** see [ACCESSIBILITY.md](ACCESSIBILITY.md). Text-only reports and requests for an alternative are welcome. A diagnosis or screenshot is never required.
- **Security:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post exploit details or credentials publicly.
- **Conduct concerns:** follow the [code of conduct reporting process](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#reporting-a-concern). Do not post incident details or personal information in public issues.

A [blank issue](https://github.com/mickpletcher/AlpacaTrading/issues/new) is available if a form is difficult to use. Include the affected component, what you expected, and what happened. Operating system, Python or PowerShell version, and browser details can help when relevant.

## Protect your data

Repository issues, pull requests, attachments, and logs are public. Use synthetic records. Remove credentials, account identifiers, balances, private trading records, personal information, local usernames, and secret-bearing URLs before sharing logs or screenshots. Never attach `.env`, databases, or real journal exports.

For unexpected orders, follow [operations and recovery](OPERATIONS.md#unexpected-order-or-position) and review the paper account directly. Do not wait for an issue response before stopping unsafe automation. Account access, billing, and provider incidents belong with the relevant provider's official support channel.
